Welcome to Cedrick & JC Kakanin

Cedrick & JC Kakanin is a family-owned business that specializes in making traditional Filipino rice cakes (kakanin). We offer a variety of kakanin that are perfect for any occasion. Our products are made with the finest ingredients and are guaranteed to be fresh and delicious. We take pride in our kakanin and are committed to providing our customers with the best quality products.

Our Mission, Vision, and Objectives

Mission

Cedrick and JC Kakanin is dedicated to sharing the authentic flavors of Philippine culinary heritage through delectable and affordable kakanin. We craft our delicacies using traditional recipes and quality ingredients to deliver genuine, delicious representations of our cultural roots for all to enjoy.

Vision

We aspire to be a beloved local brand that makes savoring authentic Filipino kakanin an accessible experience. By offering genuine yet affordable flavors, we aim to bridge generations through the universal language of delicious heritage cuisine, instilling pride and appreciation for our vibrant culinary traditions.

Objectives/Goals

Our primary goals are twofold: to expand our reach by establishing additional branches in other locations, allowing us to introduce more communities to the authentic and mouthwatering flavors of our Filipino delicacies; and to consistently provide delicious, high-quality cuisine that proudly showcases the rich heritage and diversity of Philippine cuisine through our meticulously crafted kakanin and other delectable offerings in our local area.
