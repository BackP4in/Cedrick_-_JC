What We Are

Entrepreneurship runs in our family's blood. With a passion for business, Mrs. Marites and Mr. Elmar Musa initially ventured into the clothing industry. However, that endeavor didn't quite resonate with them, so they pivoted to selling vegetables for a decade. In 2015, my grandparents decided to embark on a new journey and started a food business.

What began as a modest endeavor selling traditional Filipino delicacies like puto and kutsinta quickly blossomed into a thriving enterprise. The turning point came when we embraced innovation and expanded our offerings to include other delectable treats like sapin-sapin and sticky kutsinta. This diversification, coupled with our commitment to quality and authenticity, captured the hearts and palates of our customers.

Recognizing the power of social media, we established an online presence on Facebook, showcasing our mouthwatering creations and allowing us to reach a broader audience. Word of our delicious offerings spread rapidly, and our business experienced remarkable growth, fueled by the insatiable demand for our authentic Filipino delicacies.

Today, our family-owned business stands as a testament to the perseverance, adaptability, and unwavering dedication to culinary excellence that has been passed down through generations. With each bite, we strive to transport our customers on a journey through the rich tapestry of Filipino cuisine, honoring our heritage while continuously innovating and delighting taste buds.
